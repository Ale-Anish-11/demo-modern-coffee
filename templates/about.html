Bean To Cup

Ethically Sourced, Artfully Roasted

At Modern Coffee Shop, we believe every cup of coffee is a communion between nature, the farmer, the roaster, and the barista. We partner directly with organic coffee farmers across Nuwakot, Gulmi, and Ilam to curate only the finest 100% Arabica shade-grown cherries.

Our in-house master roasters calibrate flame temperature and drum velocity in small micro-batches every morning at 6:30 AM to bring forth delicate floral highlights, rich dark cacao, and butter caramel sweetness.
